Which account should I use?

Your own username and password. Josephus’s address is only a contact for starting a conversation. This account is separate from your Telegram account.

Why Everyhub Chat?

Josephus runs this chat on a server he rents from Hetzner, so we control our chat service and can add our own features. It uses open-source Matrix and Element.

Use encrypted conversations. People in a chat keep their own copies. If everyone agrees to invite the translation assistant, it can read that room to translate or transcribe messages on our server. Mobile notifications may still use Apple or Google services.
