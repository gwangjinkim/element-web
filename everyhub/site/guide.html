LANGUAGES & LEARNING

Understand each other.
Learn along the way.

Translate conversations and read recorded messages when you cannot listen.

1. Invite the assistant

Add @translate:chat.everyhub.org to your conversation. Agree with the other participants first: the assistant can read this room, and its replies are visible to everyone here. Processing runs on our server.

In Element X, open the conversation, tap its name at the top, then choose Invite people (sometimes under People or Members). Enter the full account above and confirm. Adding someone to a direct message may create a new group conversation: continue there with your friend and the assistant. Wait for the assistant to join, then send !help.

2. Each person chooses a language

In an English/Russian conversation, you send !language en and your friend sends !language ru. English messages receive Russian translations, and Russian messages receive English translations.

Language codes: en English · de German · ko Korean · uk Ukrainian · ru Russian · pl Polish · el Greek.

3. Turn on learning mode

Send !learn on to include both the original and the translation in replies into your language. Send !learn off for translation-only replies. Original messages remain in the conversation in either mode.

Original (en)

Good morning, my friend.

Translation (en → ru)

Доброе утро, мой друг.

4. Read voice and video messages

Send !transcribe on to automatically transcribe recordings you upload. Your friend can enable it for their recordings too. Or reply to a voice/video message with !transcribe for a one-time transcript.

Audio language is detected automatically. Transcripts also receive translations for participants who selected another supported language. Recordings must be under 20 MiB and 10 minutes. Live calls are not recorded or transcribed.

Your controls

!bot off / !bot on
Silence or resume the assistant for everyone in this conversation. Translations and transcripts stop, including one-time requests. Language choices are saved. Any participant can use this switch. Resume applies to new messages; messages sent while off are not translated later. Help and settings remain available while off.
!settings
See your choices for this conversation.
!auto off / !auto on
Stop or enable automatic translations into your language.
!transcribe off
Stop automatic transcripts of your own recordings.
!forget
Remove your saved preferences for this conversation.
!translate en ru Hello
Translate once. You can also reply to text with !translate ru en.
!help
Show all commands in chat.

Settings belong to each participant and each conversation. The same commands work in the web app, Android, and iPhone. Translations and transcripts can make mistakes; check important details. Automatic text translation supports up to 3,000 characters per message. Longer transcripts remain readable. If several people share a target language, learning mode includes the original when any of them enables it.
